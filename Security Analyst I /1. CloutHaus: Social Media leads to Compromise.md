# Incident Report
## Background
### User: Afomiya Storm  

### Type of Incident: Phishing email  

## Executive Summary
On April 3rd 2025, Afomiya storm was sent a phishing email from collabs@dior-partners.com impersonating the company Dior and offering a sponsorship deal with the victim. She then clicked the link https://super-brand-offer[.]com/login in the email at 2025-04-03 11:20:00 and provided her login credentials to the website. User her login info the attacker was able to get into both her brand Instagram account to ask her fans for money via messages and her work email. The attacker logged in from the IP 182.45.67.89 which is based out of China. Looking at the attacker's searches when logged into the victim's account it looks like they were trying to find the her exact home address as they could have found her Apartments via OSINT from a picture she posted on her social media. The attacker was also looking to find the victim's venmo information. The attacker then forwarded the user's confidential emails that included her direct deposit information, passport scam and her bank statements to the email noreply@influencer-deals.net.
